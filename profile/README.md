# Sleepimon

**Led by [Chi-Kuang Yeh](https://chikuang.github.io/) at Georgia State University.**

Sleepimon is our research group's GitHub home for collaborative research, statistical software, and reproducible analyses. We bring together students and collaborators working on statistics, machine learning, and applications across the sciences.

## Group leader

[Chi-Kuang Yeh (葉啓光)](https://cas.gsu.edu/profile/chi-kuang-yeh/) is an Assistant Professor in Statistics in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Georgia State University. He is also a Brains & Behavior faculty member in GSU's Neuroscience Institute and an affiliated faculty member of its Center for Cosmic Ray Studies.

He earned his Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Waterloo in 2023, following an M.Sc. in Statistics (2018) and an honours B.Sc. in Combined Mathematics and Statistics (2016) from the University of Victoria. Before joining GSU, he held a CANSSI-supported postdoctoral appointment at McGill University jointly with the University of Waterloo.

## Research

- **Statistical methodology:** functional data analysis, time series, dependence modeling, and high-dimensional inference.
- **Experimental design:** optimal designs for efficient, informative scientific experiments.
- **Machine learning and AI:** statistical machine learning, LLM alignment and evaluation, and retrieval-augmented generation with uncertainty quantification.

Applications span drug discovery and development, toxicology, neuroscience, cosmic ray studies, and sports. Our work connects statistical theory with practical, reproducible computational tools.
