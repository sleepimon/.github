## Hello from our little research den! 👋

Welcome to **Sleepimon**, the GitHub home of our research group at Georgia State University! This is where students and collaborators turn “what if?” into statistical methods, useful software, and reproducible research. Bring your questions, share your code, and pull up a chair. Our little blue panther has already claimed the pillow. 🐱💙

## What keeps us curious? 🔎

| Our question | Where it takes us |
| --- | --- |
| 📈 **What can complex data tell us?** | Functional data analysis, time series, dependence modeling, and high-dimensional inference. |
| 🎯 **How can we learn more from an experiment?** | [Optimal experimental design](https://chikuang.github.io/resources/opt_design/), from efficient experiments to group testing. |
| 🤖 **How can we make AI more reliable?** | Statistical machine learning, LLM alignment and evaluation, and [retrieval-augmented generation with uncertainty quantification](https://chikuang.github.io/resources/rag_system/). |

Our questions travel far: 💊 drug discovery, 🧪 toxicology, 🧠 neuroscience, 🌌 cosmic rays, and 🏀 sports. We enjoy connecting statistical ideas with real scientific problems—and building tools others can use.
